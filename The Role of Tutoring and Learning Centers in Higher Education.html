The Role of Tutoring and Learning Centers in Higher Education

Understand how tutoring and learning centers support student success, retention, and institutional goals in higher education.

📚 Course Content

This module explores the role tutoring and learning centers play in student achievement, campus culture, and equitable access to academic support.

📊 Training Presentation

Topics covered:

  • History and purpose of tutoring and learning centers
  • How centers support retention, persistence, and completion
  • Equity, access, and inclusive student support practices
  • Collaboration with faculty and campus departments
  • Data, assessment, and demonstrating impact
  • Professional roles and responsibilities of peer tutors
  • Center contributions to institutional learning goals
